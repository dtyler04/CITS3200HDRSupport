Assessment Guidelines
Creating Assessments:
  • Title: Brief, descriptive name for the assessment
  • Description: Detailed requirements and expectations
  • Degree Code: Target specific degree program (8 characters)
  • Due Week: Week number (1-52) when assessment is due
Timeline Integration:

Assessments will appear on student progression timelines at the specified week. Students will see these as milestones in their degree journey.
